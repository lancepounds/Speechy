Speechy — demonstration guide

A personal AAC phraseboard, written for an adult communicator. Speechy prioritizes meaningful, ready-to-say language and the user's own voice.

  1. Speak immediately: choose Quick answers, open a topic, and tap a phrase.
  2. Build a message: choose Tap builds, combine phrases or add-ons, then Say it.
  3. Find useful phrases: preview topics, star spoken phrases, use Used most, or switch navigation styles.
  4. Communicate without sound: use Show big or the customizable Card.
  5. Personalize: use Organize and Voice to customize the board and speech rate.

A personal communication prototype, not a standardized AAC assessment. Speech is produced by the browser/device. On the public website, edits stay in this browser; AI sentence suggestions require a supported runtime.
